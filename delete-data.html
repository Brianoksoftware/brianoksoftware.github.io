Request data deletion — Daily Bible Friend Offline

Developer: Brian Onyango Kabonyo · App: Daily Bible Friend Offline

Daily Bible Friend Offline does not require an account. Most app data stays only on your device. Use the steps below to delete what the app stores, and to request deletion of anything you sent us.

How to request deletion

  1. Delete data on your device: uninstall Daily Bible Friend Offline. This removes bookmarks, reading list items, theme preference, local Pro unlock flag, and local search-count data stored by the app (subject to your device’s normal uninstall behavior).
  2. Email a deletion request (optional, for anything you sent us) to kabonyobrian@gmail.com. Include “Daily Bible Friend Offline” and “data deletion” in the subject.
  3. We will confirm and delete personal information you emailed us (such as your email address and message content) within 30 days, unless we must keep a limited record for legal or security reasons.
Quick request: Email kabonyobrian@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • On-device app data after uninstall (bookmarks, reading list, theme, local Pro flag, local search quota)
  • Personal information you sent to our support email, when you request deletion

What may be kept / controlled by others

  • Google Play purchases: purchase records for Bible Friend Pro are kept by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. Manage or request account-related deletion through your Google Account tools.
  • Advertising (AdMob, free users): ad-related identifiers are handled by Google. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings, and review Google’s ad/privacy controls.
  • Bible text providers (for live online lookups): temporary technical data such as IP address may be processed by those services under their own policies. We do not run a user account database for the app.

Retention

We do not keep a server-side copy of your bookmarks, search history, or reading list. Support emails you send are kept only as long as needed to respond, and are deleted within 30 days of a deletion request (or earlier when no longer needed).
